Words Invaders - Mobile Application

Mobile game like Space Invaders where you're attacked by falling words — shoot them before they hit you!

Dart Flutter

Overview

Words Invaders (internally codenamed "Sparola") is a mobile arcade game that reimagines the classic Space Invaders formula: instead of alien ships, words fall from the top of the screen in three columns, and the player has to type each one correctly before it reaches the bottom. Get a word right and it explodes in a burst of animated particles; let one slip through and you lose a life.

The game is built as a single Flutter application written entirely in Dart, targeting Android and iOS with a retro '80s pixel-art aesthetic: an animated star field background, neon green/yellow/blue color palette, monospace fonts, and horizontally-drifting alien sprites on the home screen.

Difficulty ramps up across 9 progressive levels — both the word length (from 2-3 letters up to 10+) and the falling/spawn speed increase as the player advances, while a persistent high score and high level are saved locally between sessions.

Key features

  • Type-to-destroy gameplay: falling words are cleared by typing them correctly on an integrated virtual keyboard, with live highlighting of the letters already matched.
  • 9 progressive levels with increasing word length and difficulty, driven by timers that shorten the word-spawn interval and fall speed as the player levels up.
  • Scoring system worth 20 points per completed word, with automatic level-up thresholds and a celebratory "LEVEL UP" pause screen between levels.
  • 3 lives, lost whenever a word reaches the bottom of the screen unsolved, ending the run when they run out.
  • Custom-painted explosion and starfield effects (via CustomPainter) — particle bursts with simulated gravity and randomized color/duration play whenever a word is destroyed.
  • Persistent high score and high level, stored on-device with shared_preferences and shown on both the home screen and the game-over screen.
  • Sound effects (laser/shoot sound on each keystroke) via audioplayers, paired with a fully custom retro pixel-art UI for the home, in-game HUD, and game-over screens.

Tech stack

Dart

Core language for all game logic: state management, word-spawning and scoring rules, level progression, and the timer-driven game loop.

Flutter

Cross-platform UI framework used to build the entire app — home, game, and game-over screens, animations, and the virtual keyboard — from a single Android/iOS codebase.

CustomPainter

Used to hand-draw the animated star field and the particle-based explosion/laser effects directly on the canvas for smooth, lightweight visuals.

shared_preferences

Persists the player's high score and highest level reached locally on the device between game sessions.

audioplayers

Plays the game's sound effects (laser/typing sound) in sync with player input during a run.
